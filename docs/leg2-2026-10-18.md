## Arithmetic, tolerances, and applicability

Here `T` is transmitted power fraction and `R` is reflected power fraction; both are dimensionless. A relative error is a fraction of the compared magnitude. The maximum is taken over all registered spectral samples, never just the central wavelength.

| Invariant | Recomputed error | Lawful case and data requirements | Actual result |
|---|---|---|---|
| V2-a: energy conservation | `max(abs(R + T - 1))` | Only declared closed lossless `empty`/`slab`/`interface` family, every exit monitored; guided N8/G8 explicitly rejected even with misleading closed-case flags | Empty: error 0, passed |
| V2-b: passivity and finiteness | Largest of `0`, `-R`, `R-1`, `-T`, `T-1`; every entry must also be finite | Every configuration, provided its own R and T readouts exist | Empty: bound violation 0, passed |
| V2-c: symmetric-source directional power | `max(abs(forward_power / backward_power - 1))` | Symmetric empty or slab case, both symmetric-case and symmetric-source declarations, matched nonempty positive power arrays | Real measurement dropped under stop condition 1 |
| V2-d: reciprocity after source/monitor exchange | `max(abs(forward - reverse) / max(abs(forward), abs(reverse)))`; both-zero pair has error 0 | Linear reciprocal case, registered exchanged ports matching measured port identities, matching paired arrays | Real measurement dropped under stop condition 1 |

Each executed invariant has exactly one tolerance, **1e-12 absolute dimensionless error**, in the archived input config. Its stated basis is a float64 arithmetic budget for a deterministic matched empty-domain normalization control, not a fitted scattering-accuracy allowance. Finiteness is unconditional. Neither tolerance was changed after launch.

The config SHA-256 before launch and in the archived input is:

```text

```

All numerical decisions are recomputed from the run's own `cases[].measured` values. Stored `passed`, `within_tolerance`, `finite`, relative-error claims and stored `energy_residual` are not trusted. The stored residual itself remains unchecked because it has no registered covering check. Raw fields, signed fluxes and frequency arrays are additionally retained in the untracked run's `empty-reference-monitors.npz` and `empty-sample-monitors.npz`; no reference-project runtime import is used.
