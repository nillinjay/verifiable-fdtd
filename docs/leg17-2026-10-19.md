Neither stale phrase was interpreted as a new case or a permission to change the fixture. The executor did not edit shared registration files.

One nanometre (nm) is one billionth of a metre; one micrometre (um) is one millionth of a metre; one femtosecond (fs) is 1e-15 seconds. The dimensionless smoothing resolution is s = transition_nm / dx_nm. The tooth width remains 126 nm. The domain remains 13.48 by 6 by 0.2 um, with five z cells at 40 nm, eight periods, zero shift, and duration 500 fs. Material indices, wavelength 1.54925 um, precision, scene snapping, source coordinates, monitors, absorbing-boundary rule and mode selection remain those of the copied scene and leg-16 configuration. Changing transition width also changes the smoothed source cross-section used by that same scene; no separate source model is held fixed or substituted.

T is far-plane forward guided power divided by near-plane forward guided power. R is near-plane backward guided power divided by near-plane forward guided power. Effective index n_eff is the launched mode's propagation constant in radians per micrometre multiplied by the vacuum wavelength in micrometres, divided by 2 pi. These three quantities are dimensionless. The geometry instrument's Ex, Ey and Ez refer to electric-field components sampled at their respective staggered grid positions. Its normalized edge weight is the measured derivative of the sum of material permittivities with respect to tooth width, divided by the copied instrument's ideal derivative. A normalized weight of one is the ideal value for that instrument.

The actual material tensor supplies the new raster conventions. Window positions use the copied scene's tooth placement; the values themselves come from the material tensor, not a second smoothstep implementation. Counts and occupancy sums are diagnostics of the existing smooth raster, not a replacement binary width. The run artifact records the exact sampled coordinates and all eight tooth counts, avoiding a claim that an off-grid coordinate was sampled exactly.

## Unknowns and limits

The experiment does not determine a unique cause of the original reversal. It neither proves that smoothing resolution dominates every matched comparison nor proves that its effect is three times smaller everywhere. It does not separate the effects of changing the smoothed tooth material from changing other interfaces and the source cross-section: the unchanged scene applies its transition parameter to the ordered geometry and the source section together. The normalized derivative and raster measurements document that representation, without attributing an exclusive physical mechanism. No converged tooth tolerance, continuum limit, fitted convergence order, z-mesh result, independent cross-code comparison or downstream reference-grid approval follows from this leg. Historical a/c raw material hashes are not reconstructed from unavailable raw archives. No case values or criteria are missing from the registered C1-C4 evaluation. The remaining shared C5 registration edit is explicitly outside executor ownership.
