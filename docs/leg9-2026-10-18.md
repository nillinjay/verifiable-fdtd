# Leg 9 evidence — `S1` angle-sensitivity test — 2026-10-18

Contract: `plan.md`, section `LEG 9 REGISTERED — S1 ANGLE-SENSITIVITY TEST`, committed before this
work. **Status: part 1 of 2 delivered. Part 2 (the grating run) has NOT been run.**

## The question

Leg 7 measured a drift of `0.4002` degrees over `1.50`–`1.60` µm. Removing it costs either a kernel
change with no open-source counterpart (BFAST, leg 8) or `N` runs (per-frequency, about `24` hours at
device scale). **Both are expensive, so the requirement is measured before either is chosen.**

## C1 — the closed-form slab sensitivity: result

A resonant slab has `T(theta)` in closed form, so its sensitivity is free. Using the registered slab
(`n = 3.5`, optical thickness `2.1` µm, `lambda = 1.55` µm):

| incidence angle | `T` | `T` at `+0.4002` deg | change | relative |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `0.0` deg | `0.279620` | `0.279621` | `+1.21e-06` | `4.3e-06` |
| `8.5` deg | `0.280306` | `0.280387` | `+8.07e-05` | `2.9e-04` |
| `14.5` deg | `0.282369` | `0.282589` | `+2.20e-04` | `7.8e-04` |

Worst case over `1.50`–`1.62` µm at `8.5` degrees: **`max |dT| = 2.40e-03` absolute** (0.24
percentage points).

**Compared against this project's tolerances:**

| tolerance it must fit inside | value | `2.40e-03` is |
|---|---|---|
| analytic-case tolerance (leg 3, 4) | `1e-02` absolute | `4.2x` **inside** |
| the known unresolved readout discrepancy | `5.6` percentage points | `23x` **inside** |

**So for a resonant slab, `0.4` degrees of drift is well inside every tolerance this project uses.**
It is also smaller than the discrepancy we already cannot explain.

**This is the slab's sensitivity and it is NOT transferred to the grating.** A grating coupler is
sharper in angle than a slab, and by how much is exactly what criterion C2 must measure.

## C2 — the grating sensitivity: NOT DONE

Two grating Bloch runs, `0.4002` degrees apart, comparing the zero-order efficiency, have **not been
run**. Until they are, the verdict below is provisional and covers the slab only.

## C4 — provisional verdict

On the slab evidence alone the drift is **below tolerance**, which is the first of the three
pre-declared outcomes, and it would close `S1` with no implementation. **The grating decides it, so
`S1` stays open and this verdict is not final.**
