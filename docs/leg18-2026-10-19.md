## What changed

`src/parity.py`:

* `DEFAULT_PML_CELLS = 32` and a `pml_cells=DEFAULT_PML_CELLS` keyword on `project_for`. The span
  is `2 * ceil(4.0/dx) + 2 * pml_cells` and the `Region` gets `pml_cells=pml_cells`. The formula at
  the default is the old formula with `32` in the same place.
* A non-positive or non-integer depth raises `ValueError` (`True` and `48.0` included).
* The project name is unchanged at the default; a non-default depth appends `-pml<n>`, so a deeper
  run cannot be mistaken for an archived one. `geometry_assertions` keys on `'interface' in name`
  and `name.startswith('slab-n')`; both still hold.

`src/leg13_controls.py` reads `pml_cells` from the config, defaulting to `DEFAULT_PML_CELLS`,
passes it to every `project_for` call (the empty reference, the one-way cases and the soft-source
case). The depth is recorded by the archived `config.input.json` (absent means 32) and by the
resolved projects in `effective.json` (`region.pml_cells`: `[32, 32, 32]` in the C2 run, `[48, 48,
48]` in the standing run). It is not in the readout, so a default-depth readout has exactly the
leg-13 key set.

`configs/leg13-standing-controls.json` gains `"pml_cells": 48` and the rewritten `basis`. The
tolerance is untouched at `1e-06`.

`configs/leg18-standing-controls-48.json` is the standing config at 48 with a leg-18 task line;
`configs/leg18-standing-controls-default.json` is the same config with no `pml_cells` key. A test
holds both to the standing config, apart from the task line and the basis text.

Nothing else in `src/` calls `project_for` with a depth, so legs 2, 3, 4, 5, 12 and the leg-13
readout ledger build exactly what they built before.

## C1 — the control passes at 48 cells

Run `20261019T092122Z-260867`, and again under the standing config's own name in
`20261019T092126Z-260987` (323 of 323 numeric leaves identical between the two). All three cases
pass, the negative control fails as it must, `ledger diff=match`, `0 failures`. Shape `[1030, 6, 1]`
against `[998, 6, 1]`: 16 extra cells at each end, interior, source, monitors, time step and step
count unchanged (`checks/test_leg18_standing_controls.py`, `DeeperAbsorber`).

Per lossless case, measured:

* `control-slab`: `1.6098291788502195e-07`, margin `6.211839200940718`.
* `control-interface`: `1.1383354925342815e-08`, margin `87.84756397024005`.

**Cross-check against leg 15.** Leg 15 built the same slab at 48 cells with its own copied builder
(`leg15_closure.build`) on a 41-point band and recorded `1.6098291788502195e-07`, the same value to
every digit. For the interface, leg 15 recorded `1.22e-08` at 48 cells against `1.14e-08` here. The
difference is band sampling: leg 15's maximum is at `1.275` µm, which is not on this config's 21-point
grid. On the 21 wavelengths the two grids share, the per-point residuals of leg 15's `iface-pml48`
and this run agree exactly (maximum per-point difference `0.0`), so the two builders agree and the
21-point maximum is `1.14e-08`.

The other readouts moved as a deeper absorber should: the empty-run reflection floor fell from
`7.17e-14` to `3.37e-15`, the source-power self-consistency from `6.09e-10` to `4.88e-10`.

## C4 — the basis text

The `basis` of `controls.energy_closure` in `configs/leg13-standing-controls.json` now says:

> Physics: in a lossless cell whose two exits are both fully monitored and whose transverse faces
> are periodic, T + R = 1 exactly. The tolerance is a numerical floor, not a physics allowance.
> Measured on this fixture at 48 PML cells (leg 18, run 20261019T092126Z-260987): the largest
> closure residual is 1.61e-07 (control-slab, 1.6098e-07), a margin of 6.21x against 1e-06; control-
> interface gives 1.14e-08, a margin of 87.8x. The residual is a boundary echo, not a property of
> the scheme: leg 15 measured it falling 13x per doubling of PML cells from 8 to 32 and linear in
> the reflected field amplitude, the absorber's echo returning through the structure and interfering
> at the two extraction planes. The floor is therefore fixture-bound: it belongs to this absorber
> depth and this geometry, and changing either moves it. At the 32 cells every run before leg 18
> used, the same fixture gives 7.51e-07, a margin of 1.33x (leg 18, run 20261019T092119Z-260710,
> reproducing leg 13 bit for bit). A residual above the tolerance still means a monitor no longer
> captures all of the power; a residual below it does not mean the boundary is transparent.

Where each number comes from:

* `1.61e-07`, `1.6098e-07`, `6.21x`, `1.14e-08`, `87.8x`: run `20261019T092126Z-260987`, C1 above.
* `7.51e-07`, `1.33x`: run `20261019T092119Z-260710`, C2 above.
* `1e-06`: the tolerance, unchanged.
* `13x` per doubling from `8` to `32` cells, and the linearity in the reflected amplitude: leg 15
  (`docs/leg15-2026-10-19.md`, run `20261019T055357Z-121608`). Not re-measured in this leg. Leg 18
  re-measured only the 48-cell point of that ladder, and it matched (C1).

The old text ended "rounded up to 1e-6" and cited 50 cells; both are gone. The run
`20261019T092126Z-260987` archived an earlier draft of this text that cited the superseded run ids;
the numbers in it are the same. A test
(`Basis.test_every_basis_number_is_in_the_run_report`) extracts every number from the basis and
fails if any of them is absent from this report.

## What is not claimed

* That 48 cells is the right depth for any other fixture. The floor is fixture-bound.
* That the scaling law of leg 15 holds beyond 50 cells. This leg measured one depth.
* That the leg-13 readout ledger (`src/leg13_ledger.py`) rebuilds 48-cell runs faithfully. Its
  `build_family('parity-1d', ...)` calls `project_for` without a depth, so for a 48-cell run it
  rebuilds a 32-cell project. The monitors it compares (centre and transverse size) are identical at
  both depths, so it will not raise a false failure, but the project it rebuilds is not the one that
  ran. That file is outside this leg's write list.
* Anything about runs on other GPUs or solver commits. Every comparison here is on the RTX 5070 Ti
  and fork `6fbe2ee9f718`.
