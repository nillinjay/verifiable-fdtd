## What this part is, and what it is not

**Part 1 sizes the route. Part 2 uses it.** This part is pure arithmetic: it reconstructs a Gaussian
beam from its plane-wave spectrum over the Brillouin zone, exactly as the ASM sum would, and reports
how many Bloch-cell runs (`M`) each waist needs before any FDTD is launched. **No simulation runs
here and no field result is claimed.**

`ASM` **is the Array Scanning Method**, not the angular spectrum method. The fork already provides
the latter as `torchfdtd/angular_spectrum.py`; it does a different job.

| waist `w0` | beam covers | window rule | spectrum rule | tighter | **first `M` within 1 %** | affordable |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `2.0` µm | `4.44` periods | `M > 4.44` | `M > 6.98` | **spectrum** | **`8`** | yes (budget `96`) |

The spectrum rule `M > pi w0 / d` is tighter than the window rule `M > 2 w0 / d` for **every**
positive waist, because `pi > 2`. That is not a numerical coincidence; it is arithmetic, and a unit
test asserts it across four waists.

## Cross-check against the reference project

Our transcription reproduces that project's recorded sweep to four significant figures:
`0.3194` against its `0.31937` at `M = 4`, and `4.58e-4` against its `4.582e-4` at `M = 8`. That
project's feasibility study also concluded that the spectrum rule binds. The agreement is a
cross-check on the transcription, and **is not this project's result** — the numbers here are
recomputed, not copied.

## Two findings that came out of building the control

1. **The propagating branch is larger than the first Brillouin zone here.** `k0 = 4.056 µm⁻¹` against
   `pi/d = 3.491 µm⁻¹`. So a sample span **widened** beyond the zone is *not* an error: it captures
   more of the propagating spectrum and the reconstruction improves. The wrong direction is a span
   that is too **narrow**. Both a unit test and the control record this, because the intuitive
   control ("sample the wrong span") would have been the wrong one.
2. **The error floor is the reconstruction window, not the sampling.** At `w0 = 2.0` µm the error
   stops falling at about `1e-6` once `M >= 12`. Buying more runs past that buys nothing, and the
   leg says so rather than recommending a larger `M`.

## What part 2 still has to do

1. Launch `M` Bloch cells per waist (`M = 8` at `w0 = 2.0` µm, `M = 24` at `w0 = 5.0` µm) and
   combine them by the ASM midpoint sum.
2. **Measure the single-source frequency drift** (criterion C2). That drift is the defect this leg
   exists to fix, and it has not been measured here.
3. Compare the combined far-field intensity against the arithmetic reference (criterion C3).
4. Check `T + R = 1` within the declared tolerance (criterion C4).

None of these four is done. Part 1 provides the size of the job and the tolerance they must meet.
